\subsection{Overview}

The 2026 task provides roughly one hour of labeled soundscape audio, and the leaderboard rewards
supervised pipelines fit directly to it.
Rather than chase architectural novelty, we take an Occam's-razor view: we ask how much of the
leaderboard a \emph{single} spectrogram CNN can recover, and which few design choices actually
move the public score.
Our final submission is a score-level blend of a frozen Perch v2 \cite{hamer2025perch2} probe and
a trained HGNetV2-B0 sound-event-detection (SED) network, reaching \textbf{0.929} public LB
(Figure~\ref{fig:baseline-ensemble}, Table~\ref{tab:baseline-ablation}).
The central finding of this section is that the single SED CNN already reaches \textbf{0.917}, so
the blend with Perch adds only the final \textbf{$+0.012$}.
Almost all of the gain comes from a handful of frontend and architecture choices; data
augmentation tricks, extra unlabeled data, and alternate training objectives did not transfer
(Section~\ref{sec:negatives}).

\subsection{The SED model}

The SED member is an HGNetV2-B0 backbone (ImageNet-pretrained, $6$\,M parameters) over log-mel
spectrograms computed on-GPU: $256$ mel bins, $2048$-point FFT, $313$-sample hop at $32$\,kHz over
$5$\,s windows, normalized with AmplitudeToDB (per-sample standardization, $80$\,dB floor).
A log-sum-exp (LSE) pooling head aggregates frame logits to a clip-level prediction; we train with
binary cross-entropy and spectrogram-space mixup ($\alpha{=}1.0$, $5$-epoch warmup) for $20$ epochs
under a one-cycle schedule.
We fit four folds and average their logits, then apply site- and hour-conditioned priors as
post-processing.
The frozen Perch v2 member is described above; the two are blended with equal weight in
probability space.

\subsection{Ablation ladder}

\input{sections/floats/tab_ablation_ladder}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation-ladder} traces the single-model SED system from the simplest baseline to
the deployed configuration, and then the ensemble step.
Two choices dominate.
\textbf{dB-scale mel normalization} (AmplitudeToDB) is worth $+0.034$ in a paired single-variable
test, the single largest frontend lever: it makes quiet and loud recordings comparable, which
matters in a domain where focal training clips and soundscape test clips differ sharply in level.
\textbf{Backbone choice} is the largest architecture lever: swapping EfficientNet-B0 for
HGNetV2-B0 under an otherwise fixed recipe adds $+0.037$.
On top of these, the NNAudio dB-mel frontend on HGNet reaches $0.906$ single-fold; four-fold
logit averaging adds $+0.007$ ($0.913$); and site/hour priors---pure post-processing, no
retraining---add $+0.004$ ($0.917$).
The final score-level blend with the Perch probe adds $+0.012$ to reach $0.929$.
Every step was validated by a leaderboard submission, because offline metrics did not predict LB
rank (Section~\ref{sec:negatives}).

\subsection{Why HGNetV2-B0: a parity result}

We adopt the ImageNet HGNetV2-B0 recipe because it is the \emph{simplest} route to its score, not
the only one.
Two more elaborate pipelines reach the same place: an ImageNet EfficientNet-B0 SED distilled from
Perch v2 embeddings recovers the identical $0.906$ single-fold (a $+0.050$ paired gain over its
no-distillation control), and an EfficientNetV2-S pretrained from scratch on broad Neotropical
Xeno-Canto audio reaches $0.907$ single-fold and $0.916$ at four folds (Appendix~\ref{app:parity}).
All three converge to ${\sim}0.906$ single-fold and ${\sim}0.916$ at four folds.
Since distillation requires a teacher pipeline and custom pretraining requires an extra
large-scale training stage, neither buys accuracy over the plain HGNet recipe; we read this as
evidence that the ceiling is set by the data, not the backbone.

\subsection{What does not transfer}
\label{sec:negatives}

A recurring and, we argue, generalizable finding is that \textbf{offline validation AUROC/cmAP
anti-correlate with the leaderboard} across $20{+}$ submissions---the best offline model was
repeatedly the worst on LB---so we treated every change as a single-variable experiment arbitrated
by a Kaggle submission.
Under that discipline, most ``improvements'' failed: pseudo-labeling on unlabeled soundscapes
regressed in all six attempts ($-0.005$ to $-0.074$), a $14$-change training-recipe sweep
(cross-entropy loss, waveform mixup, attention pooling heads, multi-context windows, manifold
mixup) was 0-for-14 on LB, learnable PCEN lost $-0.026$, and cross-entropy loss was catastrophic
($0.494$).
The levers that worked were few and frontend/architecture-level; the curated record is in
Appendix~\ref{app:curated} (Table~\ref{tab:curated-negatives}).
