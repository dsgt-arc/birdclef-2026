## TL;DR finding (data-driven)

The three vocalizing taxa in BirdCLEF 2026 do **not** occupy distinct frequency bands —
they **overlap heavily in 1–4 kHz**. In particular the dataset's "insects" are **cicadas**
(low-mid fundamentals), *not* high-pitched crickets/katydids, so the textbook
"insects = high band" assumption is false here. What separates the taxa is
**spectro-temporal structure**:

| Taxon | structure | peak-freq median [IQR] (kHz) | spectral-centroid median (kHz) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frogs (Amphibia) | pulsed train | 1.8 [0.7, 4.2] — wide/bimodal | 2.4 |
| Birds (Aves) | FM whistle / harmonic sweep | 2.9 [1.9, 3.7] | 3.3 |
| Insects (Insecta) | sustained broadband drone | 1.8 [1.6, 2.3] — low & tight | 2.0 |

*(per-recording peak frequency, n = 80 random recordings per taxon; see Methods.)*

**Paper takeaway:** frequency band alone cannot separate the taxa → motivates
spectrogram CNNs (which learn spectro-temporal structure) over hand-crafted band features.

## Dataset facts used (BirdCLEF 2026 `train.csv` / `taxonomy.csv`)

- 234 target classes: **Aves 162, Amphibia 35, Insecta 28, Mammalia 8, Reptilia 1**.
- Train recordings/taxon: **Aves 34,799, Amphibia 451, Insecta 199, Mammalia 99, Reptilia 1**.

- Insecta = 3 identified species (*Guyalna cuta*, *Quesada gigas* = Giant Cicada,
  *Prionacris erosa*) + 25 unlabeled "Insect sonotype" placeholders → cicada-dominated.

## Methods (reproducible)

- **Clips:** loudest 5 s window (frame-RMS proxy) of the highest-rated recording per chosen
  species; saved as FLAC under `notebooks/assets/clips/<taxon>/`.
- **Spectrograms:** librosa STFT `n_fft=1024`, `hop_length=256`; `amplitude_to_db(ref=max)`;
  display range [−80, 0] dB; `magma`; linear frequency 0–16 kHz.
- **Mean spectrum (Fig 2 left):** SciPy Welch `nperseg=2048`, `fs=32000`; each file's PSD
  normalized to sum 1 (compare *shape*, not loudness); mean over 80 random files/taxon;
  9-bin moving-average smoothing for display only.
- **Peak frequency (Fig 2 right):** Welch `nperseg=4096`, argmax over `f ≥ 300 Hz`
  (ignore DC/rumble); 80 random files/taxon; RNG seed 51.
