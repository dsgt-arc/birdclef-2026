- **Current live baseline, not an idea to retest blindly:** the validated fast-proxy incumbent is now `K_SITE=6` + `site_blend_anchor_lambda=1.1e-3` + `site_blend_lr_mult=5.3` + the bounded per-site trust term on the *dynamic cross-attention update magnitude*. The key lesson is coupled retuning after the site-bank capacity reduction: shrinking the site bank from 8→6 prototypes improved the proxy materially, moved the anchor optimum far upward from the old `6e-4` neighborhood into the `~1e-3` range, and then reopened the blend-LR axis upward so `5.3×` now beats both the old `5×` and a too-large `5.5×`. Future micro-gains in this regime should be validated with reruns rather than assumed bit-for-bit deterministic.
- **Focal-subset construction is still load-bearing and quantized.** `FOCAL_CACHE_SIZE=17408` remains dramatically better than nearby sizes, and a supposedly high-coverage `FOCAL_CACHE_SEED=104` draw regressed badly. If revisiting subset search, do it empirically on the proxy or with a targeted objective tied to bird/with_focal balance; naive species/family-coverage heuristics are stale.
- **Static star-schema affinity is firmly a reporting artifact, not a forward-pass feature.** Both direct uses of the explicit species×site affinity `A(s,l)` failed badly: a zero-init logit-additive prior regressed hard, and a numerically safe near-identity convex blend also regressed hard. Treat `A(s,l)` as interpretability-only for now.
- **Several star-schema architectural knobs are now effectively pruned.** Full multiplicative FiLM replacement of the cross-attention residual regressed and failed checks; direct low-rank site-conditioned species-temperature modulation was either deadlocked at zero-init or harmful once activated; feeding `site_head.W_K(M_site)` into cross-attention catastrophically regressed; an attended-driven site-dependent internal gate also regressed hard; a raw site-summary query residual regressed and failed checks; broad norm/amplitude changes (LayerNorm on attended residual, negative gate-init bias, global residual scale) are stale as well. Future architecture work should therefore avoid reusing the direct site-retrieval geometry, modifying the internal gate, adding raw query-side site biases, or leaning on broad modulation knobs.
- **Direct site-retrieval trust scalars are now mostly stale.** Both the simple detached site-uncertainty scalar and a richer per-site site-margin confidence signal either tied or regressed slightly, usually by over-rotating toward without_focal/amphibian/insect at the expense of bird/with_focal. The one dynamic trust signal that *did* help was derived from the composition path itself (`||M_cond - M_shared||`), not from direct `M_site` retrieval.
- **The update-magnitude trust path is narrower than it first looked — and still genuinely useful.** RMS update magnitude helps a little, but centering it, replacing it with L1, adding output disagreement, adding update peakiness, or just speeding up its optimizer all regressed; a simple monotone rescale (`log1p`) was exactly inert. Moving the same signal earlier into the prototype-conditioning path was also slightly worse, and a fresh ablation on the fully retuned incumbent confirmed that removing the term still regresses slightly. So the useful information appears to be exactly the plain detached RMS update magnitude, used specifically at the output-level fallback.
- **The remaining architectural headroom is likely *outside* trust-statistic elaboration.** If continuing on architecture, prefer a different family of identity-at-init composition changes rather than piling more summary scalars onto alpha or relocating the same scalar earlier in the path. The evidence now says the trust path has one narrow useful signal and little slack beyond it, the older strong-site-dropout regularizer is fully obsolete on top of the trust baseline, and the old global regularization optimums still hold (`attn_dropout=0.10` remains best).
- **Capacity tuning is newly live, but very asymmetric.** Reducing the site bank from `K_SITE=8` to `K_SITE=6` was the first materially better change in a while, and nearby sweeps pinned it sharply: `K_SITE=5` collapsed hard and `K_SITE=7` gave back too much of the hard-subset lift. Species-bank capacity is now pinned too: `K_SPECIES=7`, `9`, and `10` all failed, with larger values also pushing the peak later. So the useful capacity signal is specifically "smaller site bank," not "more prototype capacity everywhere." The good regime is also *coupled*: once `K_SITE=6` is in place, the old anchor/blend-LR conclusions from the `K_SITE=8` era are obsolete.
- **The Perch-logit side branch is still deferred but not dead.** The current 14,795-d path is operationally wrong because cache → DataLoader → GPU transport times out. Revisit only after compressing logits at cache-build time (for example BirdCLEF-species-mapped logits or another compact summary) so the extra signal is cheap enough for the fast proxy.
- **Several newly-tried orthogonal regularization ideas are now stale too.** Site-aware mixup on the tiny strong soundscape stream regressed badly even at very mild `alpha=0.05`, so synthetic interpolation of the scarce site-conditioned labeled scaffold is a poor fit here. Likewise, giving the per-species Platt head a faster optimizer track (`cal_lr_mult=3`) strongly over-rotated the model toward without_focal/amphibian/insect and away from bird/with_focal. Uniformly rescaling or softening the weak focal stream also failed on both sides: downweighting its BCE (`weak_focal_weight=0.9`), upweighting it (`1.05`), and selectively increasing its label smoothing (`weak_label_smoothing=0.02`) all lost too much bird/with_focal. A modest site-head-specific LR increase (`site_head_lr_mult=1.5`) also regressed, so the smaller `K_SITE=6` site table is not just under-optimized. These are no longer good next bets.
- **The site-blend prior itself is now pinned too.** After the `K_SITE=6` / stronger-anchor / faster-blend retune, the old 18% fallback prior still beats both a more shared-heavy target (`site_blend_anchor_prob=0.20`) and a more site-conditioned target (`0.16`). So the remaining site-blend headroom is not in prior-probability retuning; strength/LR were the useful knobs, and those now look solved.
- **Holdout-site shared-trust variants are now mostly pruned.** Three increasingly thesis-faithful attempts all failed to beat the incumbent: (1) a fully shared update-magnitude slope nearly tied but was slightly too global, (2) a shared-plus-residual decomposition over-smoothed the hard-subset lift, and (3) a selective unseen-site shared fallback trained via occasional shared-path windows proved effectively inert even with explicit anchors and a dedicated fast LR. Keep the high-level lesson — unseen sites *want* some principled fallback — but do not keep spending cycles on the current shared-slope formulation unless a genuinely new coupling mechanism appears.
- **Pre-peak unsupervised/site pressure is still effectively dead on this proxy.** Even on the improved `K_SITE=6` + `site_blend_anchor_lambda=1.1e-3` + `site_blend_lr_mult=5.3` baseline, starting the unlabeled/site warmup before the step-300 peak (even gently, with `λ_site=λ_xattn=0.01` and a long 250→900 ramp) collapsed the proxy and moved the peak earlier to step 200. A post-300 freeze of the projection + species bank was harmless, but even coupling that freeze with phase-2 unsupervised warmup remained exactly inert. If we want the thesis's unlabeled soundscape stream to matter, it likely needs a different selection protocol or a very different objective—not just schedule tricks around the existing losses.
