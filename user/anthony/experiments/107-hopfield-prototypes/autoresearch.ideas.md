- Delayed strong-site dropout on labeled soundscapes: the p=0.25 always-on variant improved `without_focal` and insect AUROC a lot but hurt overall and peaked at step 200. Try activating species-only routing only after the site/xattn warmup, or at a smaller rate (e.g. 0.05–0.10), so unlabeled/site-conditioned structure forms first and the anti-overfit regularizer comes later.
- If increasing unlabeled cache size again, pair it with a compensating species-side anchor (e.g. slightly larger focal cache, stronger/longer focal exposure, or weaker xattn/site lambdas) so the extra unlabeled coverage does not collapse bird/with_focal performance while boosting amphibian/without_focal/insect.
- New sharded-cache finding: unsup cache size 32k → 65k became almost perfectly inert on the current best schedule. Likely cause is the recording-grouped unsup batch sampler / early-stop horizon, not the cache itself. Expose `unsup_batch_recordings` and `unsup_batch_windows_per_recording` through `autoresearch.sh`, then test whether more recordings per batch or fewer windows per recording makes larger unlabeled caches actually influence the first 500–600 steps.
- Focal-subset construction is now clearly load-bearing and highly quantized: `FOCAL_CACHE_SIZE=17408` is dramatically better than nearby sizes under the same seed. Instead of smoothing it with nested PPSWOR (which regressed), try a coverage-aware subset policy or a small search over focal subset seeds/objectives that explicitly optimizes species/family coverage and avoids bird-dominant draws.
- The zero-init Perch-logit side branch is architecturally plausible but operationally wrong in its current form: streaming 14,795-d `perch_logits` through cache → DataLoader → GPU times out even after sidecars are built. Revisit only after pre-mapping/compressing logits to the 234 BirdCLEF species (or another compact summary) at cache-build time, then feed that compressed signal to the adapter.
- Local architecture sweeps now bracket the current cross-attn/prototype geometry pretty tightly: 4 heads beats both 2 and 8; `K_species=8` beats both 6 and 12; `K_site=8` beats 4. Next architecture moves should change *how* site conditioning is applied (e.g. learned residual scale, norm/gating on the attention update, or asymmetric species-vs-site projection structure), not just raw capacity counts.
