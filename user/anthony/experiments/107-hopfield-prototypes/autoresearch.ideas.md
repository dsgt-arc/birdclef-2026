- **Current live baseline, not an idea to retest blindly:** the validated fast-proxy incumbent is now `K_SITE=6` + `site_blend_lr_mult=5` + `site_blend_anchor_lambda=1e-3` + the bounded per-site trust term on the *dynamic cross-attention update magnitude*. The trust term still uses a learned per-site coefficient on detached RMS `||M_cond - M_shared||`, but the big new change is capacity: reducing the site bank from 8→6 prototypes improved the proxy materially, and that in turn moved the anchor optimum far upward from the old `6e-4` neighborhood to about `1e-3`. A near-exact rerun of the new best showed only tiny ~1e-5 jitter, so future micro-gains in this regime should be validated with reruns rather than assumed bit-for-bit deterministic.
- **Focal-subset construction is still load-bearing and quantized.** `FOCAL_CACHE_SIZE=17408` remains dramatically better than nearby sizes, and a supposedly high-coverage `FOCAL_CACHE_SEED=104` draw regressed badly. If revisiting subset search, do it empirically on the proxy or with a targeted objective tied to bird/with_focal balance; naive species/family-coverage heuristics are stale.
- **Static star-schema affinity is firmly a reporting artifact, not a forward-pass feature.** Both direct uses of the explicit species×site affinity `A(s,l)` failed badly: a zero-init logit-additive prior regressed hard, and a numerically safe near-identity convex blend also regressed hard. Treat `A(s,l)` as interpretability-only for now.
- **Several star-schema architectural knobs are now effectively pruned.** Full multiplicative FiLM replacement of the cross-attention residual regressed and failed checks; direct low-rank site-conditioned species-temperature modulation was either deadlocked at zero-init or harmful once activated; feeding `site_head.W_K(M_site)` into cross-attention catastrophically regressed; an attended-driven site-dependent internal gate also regressed hard; a raw site-summary query residual regressed and failed checks; broad norm/amplitude changes (LayerNorm on attended residual, negative gate-init bias, global residual scale) are stale as well. Future architecture work should therefore avoid reusing the direct site-retrieval geometry, modifying the internal gate, adding raw query-side site biases, or leaning on broad modulation knobs.
- **Direct site-retrieval trust scalars are now mostly stale.** Both the simple detached site-uncertainty scalar and a richer per-site site-margin confidence signal either tied or regressed slightly, usually by over-rotating toward without_focal/amphibian/insect at the expense of bird/with_focal. The one dynamic trust signal that *did* help was derived from the composition path itself (`||M_cond - M_shared||`), not from direct `M_site` retrieval.
- **The update-magnitude trust path is narrower than it first looked.** RMS update magnitude helps a little, but centering it, replacing it with L1, adding output disagreement, adding update peakiness, or just speeding up its optimizer all regressed; a simple monotone rescale (`log1p`) was exactly inert. Moving the same signal earlier into the prototype-conditioning path was also slightly worse. So the useful information appears to be the plain detached RMS update magnitude itself, used specifically at the output-level fallback.
- **The remaining architectural headroom is likely *outside* trust-statistic elaboration.** If continuing on architecture, prefer a different family of identity-at-init composition changes rather than piling more summary scalars onto alpha or relocating the same scalar earlier in the path. The evidence now says the trust path has one narrow useful signal and little slack beyond it, the older strong-site-dropout regularizer is fully obsolete on top of the trust baseline, and the old global regularization optimums still hold (`attn_dropout=0.10` remains best).
- **Capacity tuning is newly live, but very asymmetric.** Reducing the site bank from `K_SITE=8` to `K_SITE=6` was the first materially better change in a while, and nearby sweeps pinned it sharply: `K_SITE=5` collapsed hard and `K_SITE=7` gave back too much of the hard-subset lift. In contrast, increasing species-bank capacity on top of the new site-bank optimum (`K_SPECIES=10`) catastrophically failed. So the useful capacity signal is specifically "smaller site bank," not "more prototype capacity everywhere."
- **The Perch-logit side branch is still deferred but not dead.** The current 14,795-d path is operationally wrong because cache → DataLoader → GPU transport times out. Revisit only after compressing logits at cache-build time (for example BirdCLEF-species-mapped logits or another compact summary) so the extra signal is cheap enough for the fast proxy.
