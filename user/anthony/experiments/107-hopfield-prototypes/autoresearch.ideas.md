- **Current live baseline, not an idea to retest blindly:** `site_blend_lr_mult=5` + `site_blend_anchor_lambda=6e-4` is the validated fast-proxy incumbent. The nearby anchor sweep is now well characterized: `4e-4` and `7e-4` regressed, and delaying the anchor until step 100 also regressed. Future site-blend work should treat “anchor on from step 0” as fixed and look for a *different* selective regularizer rather than more decimal-place tuning.
- **Focal-subset construction is still load-bearing and quantized.** `FOCAL_CACHE_SIZE=17408` remains dramatically better than nearby sizes, and a supposedly high-coverage `FOCAL_CACHE_SEED=104` draw regressed badly. If revisiting subset search, do it empirically on the proxy or with a targeted objective tied to bird/with_focal balance; naive species/family-coverage heuristics are stale.
- **Static star-schema affinity is now firmly a reporting artifact, not a forward-pass feature.** Both direct uses of the explicit species×site affinity `A(s,l)` failed badly: a zero-init logit-additive prior regressed hard, and a numerically safe near-identity convex blend also regressed hard. Treat `A(s,l)` as interpretability-only for now.
- **Several star-schema architectural knobs are now effectively pruned.** Full multiplicative FiLM replacement of the cross-attention residual regressed and failed checks; direct low-rank site-conditioned species-temperature modulation was either deadlocked at zero-init or harmful once activated; feeding `site_head.W_K(M_site)` into cross-attention catastrophically regressed; broad norm/amplitude changes (LayerNorm on attended residual, negative gate-init bias, global residual scale) are also stale. Future architecture work should therefore avoid reusing the direct site-retrieval geometry or broad modulation knobs.
- **The one star-schema architectural direction that was almost neutral:** a detached per-window site-uncertainty scalar used to modulate the shared-vs-conditioned fallback was essentially a tie. If future work returns to architecture, the remaining plausible space is *richer but still bounded* dynamic trust signals for site conditioning—not direct affinity priors, not temperature modulation, and not replacing the additive residual outright.
- **The Perch-logit side branch is still deferred but not dead.** The current 14,795-d path is operationally wrong because cache → DataLoader → GPU transport times out. Revisit only after compressing logits at cache-build time (for example BirdCLEF-species-mapped logits or another compact summary) so the extra signal is cheap enough for the fast proxy.
