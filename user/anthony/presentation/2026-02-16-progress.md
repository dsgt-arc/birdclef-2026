## Exp 007 — Takeaways

- Pre-training is the **dominant factor** — both conditions beat scratch by ~2×
- Condition B (128×1, Mamba-only) matches A despite re-initializing patch_embed
- **Mamba layers carry the learning** — patch tokenization is recoverable
- Variance in Condition A (0.165–0.179) suggests sensitivity to fine-tuning dynamics
- Still far from SOTA (0.55) — representation quality bottleneck remains

## Literature Review — Why MPC Fails at 128×1

:::::::::::::: {.columns}
::: {.column width="50%"}

**The problem:**

- MPC (contrastive) requires negatives to be distinguishable from positives
- With 128×1 patches, adjacent mel frames are nearly identical
- MPC accuracy stuck at random (0.002 vs 0.00128)
- **Audio-MAE** found adding NCE to MSE has **no benefit**

:::
::: {.column width="50%"}

**The solution (Yadav et al. 2025):**

- MSE-only reconstruction validated for Mamba
- Mamba outperforms Transformers: **87.9 vs 68.2** aggregated across 10 audio tasks
- Bird-MAE achieves **55 cmAP** on BirdSet HSN with pure MSE + ViT-Large
- → Our approach: MPG (reconstruction only), no contrastive

:::
::::::::::::::

## Exp 009 — Pretraining Architecture

:::::::::::::: {.columns}
::: {.column width="50%"}

**MPG (Masked Patch Generation):**

- Reconstruct masked mel patches
- **Span masking**: Uniform[5, 15] tokens (50–150ms)
- Contiguous spans preserve Mamba state in visible regions
- 78% masking ratio

:::
::: {.column width="50%"}

**Training augmentations:**

- **Grid jitter**: 0–5 frame (0–50ms) random temporal offset
- **Per-patch normalization**: predict spectral shape, not loudness
- MPC (contrastive) removed — degenerate with 128×1
- JiTTER (shuffle) removed — fights Mamba's sequential dynamics

:::
::::::::::::::

## Exp 009 — Mini-grid Ablation

- 3 conditions, 3/10 epochs completed (timed out at 2h)
- ~28 samples/sec, ~40 min/epoch (911 steps × batch 64)

| Condition | Loss | Decoder | val MSE | val L1 |
|:----------|:----:|:-------:|:-------:|:------:|
| A | MSE | MLP (2-layer) | 0.371 | 0.450 |
| G | L1 | MLP (2-layer) | 0.371 | 0.446 |
| **H** | **MSE** | **Linear** | **0.356** | **0.439** |

- **H (linear decoder) leads on both cross-metrics** after 3 epochs
- SimMIM philosophy confirmed: encoder absorbs complexity when decoder is simple
- A vs G tied — MSE and L1 loss functions produce equivalent representations early on

## Exp 010 — BamBird Bug Hunt

**Initial run: zero ROIs across all 38,000 files**

Three stacked bugs in custom scikit-maad pipeline (all masked by broad `try/except`):

1. Missing `median_equalizer(Sxx)` before dB conversion — raw power ≪ 1 made all dB negative
2. Wrong kwargs: `bin_h`/`bin_l` (absolute mode) instead of `bin_std`/`bin_per` (relative mode)
3. `select_rois()` returns `(im, df)` tuple — pixel coords need `format_features(df, tn, fn)`

**Fix**: replaced custom maad code with full **bambird library** 3-stage pipeline:

- Stage 1: `bambird.single_file_extract_rois()` — bandpass + ROI detection + .wav export
- Stage 2: `bambird.compute_features()` — wavelet shape features per ROI
- Stage 3: `bambird.find_cluster()` — per-species DBSCAN (signal vs noise)

After fix: **~89 ROIs/file** on test recording, all tests passing
